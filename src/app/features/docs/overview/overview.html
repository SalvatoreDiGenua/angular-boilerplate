Alignment policy

Stay close to Angular.

The boilerplate targets the latest supported Angular major and adopts stable modern APIs as they mature. Experimental APIs are documented separately rather than silently becoming project defaults.

Follow Angular roadmap

Documentation pages are examples, not business features. Delete or replace them when starting a real application.
